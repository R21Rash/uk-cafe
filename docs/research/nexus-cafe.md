# Nexus Cafe Research

Researched on 2026-10-05 for an unofficial MintBox Studio concept demo.

## Confirmed public details

- Name: Nexus cafe / Nexus Cafe.
- Address: 38 Meriden Street, Birmingham B5 5LS.
- Phone: +44 7742 149976.
- Urbanary lists rating 4.8 from 57 reviews.
- Urbanary describes it as a neighbourhood cafe in Digbeth / Meriden Street and lists known-for signals including strong coffee, welcoming regulars, and relaxed neighbourhood vibe.
- Independent Birmingham describes Nexus as a proudly independent WOC-owned speciality coffee shop on Meriden Street in Digbeth.
- Independent Birmingham member-offer copy references "any bagel + a hot drink from the main menu" and "10% off the bill" as offer/menu signals.
- WorldCafeMap lists Nexus Cafe as a speciality coffee cafe with 4.8 from 79 reviews, known for latte, bagel, and matcha. It summarises recent Google review themes around service, cleanliness, ambiance, and food and drink quality.
