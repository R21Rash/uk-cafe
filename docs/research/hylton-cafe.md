# Hylton Cafe Research

Researched on 2026-10-05 for an unofficial MintBox Studio concept demo.

## Confirmed public details

- Name: Hylton / The Hylton Cafe.
- Address: 2 Hylton Street, Birmingham B18 6HN.
- Phone: 0121 554 7005.
- Category signals: British cafe, breakfast and brunch.
- Public reputation signals: Tripadvisor lists 4.7 from 306 reviews; Sluurpy lists 4.6/5 and references Google, Facebook, Yelp, and TripAdvisor aggregates; Restaurant Guru lists Google 4.7 and Trip 4.7.
- Tripadvisor lists cuisines as Cafe and British, meal types as breakfast, lunch, and brunch, and special diet signals including vegetarian friendly, vegan options, and gluten free options.
- Tripadvisor lists features including delivery, highchairs, outdoor seating, seating, table service, and takeout.
- Sluurpy popular dish signals include Full English Breakfast, Eggs Benedict, Latte, Salmon N Scrambled Eggs, Burger & Curly Fries, Hot Chocolate with Marshmallows, Espresso, Veggie B'Fast, Cheese on Toast, and Tea N Toast.
- Restaurant Guru mentions British cuisine, sausages, bacon, mushrooms, pudding, English coffee, tea, and latte.
