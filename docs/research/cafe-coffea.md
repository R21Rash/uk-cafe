# Cafe Coffea Research

Researched on 2026-10-05 for an unofficial MintBox Studio concept demo.

## Confirmed public details

- Name: Cafe Coffea / CAFE COFFEA LTD.
- Address: 8 Burney Lane, Birmingham B8 2AH.
- Companies House status: active private limited company.
- SIC: 56102, unlicensed restaurants and cafes.
- Restaurant Guru lists Google rating 4.3/5, 425 ratings, and opening hours including Wednesday closed.
- Restaurant Guru mentions lamb, shakshuka, avocado toast, French toast, croissants, Spanish latte, karak, and mint tea.
- Restaurant Guru notes wheelchair accessibility and mentions stylish interior, calm atmosphere, and service comments in visitor review summaries.
- Wheree describes the address as 8 Burney Lane and summarises it as a laid-back spot with diverse menu choices and inviting decor.
- Restaurants World summarises customer praise around shakshuka, eggs benedict, coffee selections, and iced Spanish latte.
